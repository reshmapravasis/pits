Cloud-Based Accounting

Sahakari MIS is a comprehensive cloud-based accounting solution designed for Nidhi companies, societies, KMLs, and inventory-based businesses.

  • Separate logins for tracking transactions and preventing fraud.
  • MIS passbooks for real-time financial monitoring by clients.
  • Dedicated auditor portal for seamless report generation.

Seamless Collection

RIO Easy Collect replaces traditional hardware with a mobile application, enabling agents to handle daily collections paperlessly.

  • Instant SMS notifications for payment transparency.
  • Secretary dashboard for complete oversight of agent activities.
  • 100% paperless workflow reducing operational costs.

Innovative Loan Tracking

The Mulla Mithra application empowers Kudumbasree units to manage loans disbursed under the "Muttathe Mulla" government scheme.

  • Accurate tracking of loan repayments and schedules.
  • Trend monitoring for smarter disbursement decisions.
  • User-friendly UI designed for local community members.
